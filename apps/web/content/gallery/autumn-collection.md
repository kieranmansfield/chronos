# Autumn Collection

A series of photographs exploring the warmth and quiet of the autumn season — shot over several weeks across rural landscapes.

## About This Collection

This collection focuses on three qualities that make autumn light distinctive:

- **Colour temperature** — The shift from summer's cool whites to amber and copper
- **Atmosphere** — Fog, mist, and directional light that only appear when temperatures drop
- **Texture** — Bark revealed as canopies thin, stone bridges framed by foliage, vineyard rows in low sun

All images were shot on location without artificial lighting.
