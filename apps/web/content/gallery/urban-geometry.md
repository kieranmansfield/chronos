# Urban Geometry

Cities are full of unintentional composition. This collection captures the geometric patterns that emerge from architecture when you look at it as pure form — not function, not context, just shape and light.

## Process

Every image in this collection was shot handheld, without a tripod. The constraint forced quick decisions about framing and kept the approach responsive to fleeting light conditions.

The edit is deliberately restrained — minor exposure corrections and conversion to a muted, slightly cool tone that reads consistently across the set.
