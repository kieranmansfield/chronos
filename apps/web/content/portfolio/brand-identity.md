# Nocturn — Brand Identity

Sleep brands tend to fall into two camps: hyper-clinical (white coats, graphs, blue light) or aggressively cosy (clouds, sheep, pastel everything). Nocturn wanted to be neither. They make evidence-backed sleep products, but they understand that bedtime is intimate, personal, and a little ritualistic.

The brief: _make sleep feel like something you look forward to, not something you optimise._

::figure{src="https://picsum.photos/seed/brand-hero/1200/600" alt="Nocturn brand identity spread" caption="The final identity system — warm enough to feel human, structured enough to feel credible"}
::

## Starting Point

When we first spoke, the founders had a name, a handful of formulations, and a pitch deck full of competitor screenshots circled in red. They knew what they didn't want. That's a great place to start.

We spent two weeks in research — not just looking at sleep brands, but at hospitality, fragrance, and editorial design. The spaces where calm and craft overlap.

::figure{src="https://picsum.photos/seed/brand-audit/800/500" alt="Moodboard with hospitality and editorial references" caption="Early moodboard pulling from Aesop, Kinfolk, and Ace Hotel — brands that balance restraint with warmth"}
::

## The Mark

The logomark is a crescent that doubles as a closing parenthesis. It came from a sketch on a Post-it during our second session and survived every round of refinement.

Simple enough to stamp on a tin lid. Distinctive enough to recognise at thumbnail size. No gradients, no tricks.

::figure{src="https://picsum.photos/seed/brand-logo/800/400" alt="Nocturn logo across dark and light backgrounds" caption="The mark across contexts — screen, print, and emboss"}
::

## Colour and Type

The palette started with a constraint: no blue. Every sleep brand uses blue. Instead we went warm — deep charcoal-plum for depth, blush coral as the signature accent, cream that feels like good paper.

Fraunces for headlines: character without being loud, soft serifs that feel hand-touched.

## What Landed

::callout{icon="i-lucide-trending-up" color="success"}
Within three months of launch, organic social mentions doubled and DTC conversion hit 4.2% — roughly 3× the category average.
::
